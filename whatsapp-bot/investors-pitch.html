WhatsApp Commerce Platform

Turning WhatsApp Chats into Orders & Bookings

A WhatsApp-first commerce and service-booking platform for modern businesses

🚀 The Opportunity

WhatsApp is already the primary sales and customer interaction channel for millions of businesses.

However, orders, payments, and service bookings are still handled manually, limiting scalability and repeat business.

📉 The Problem

Unstructured Selling & Booking

Businesses manage product orders, service bookings, and payments inside chat messages with no system.

Expensive Automation Tools

Most WhatsApp automation solutions rely on costly APIs or enterprise SaaS pricing.

💡 Our Solution

A unified WhatsApp commerce and service booking management platform that converts chats into structured orders, payments, and appointments using a single admin dashboard.

⚙️ Platform Scope

📊 Business Dashboard

Revenue trends, order status, booking status, top products, and recent activity.

🛒 Orders & Payments

Product orders via WhatsApp, admin-created orders, manual payment tracking for UPI, cash, or bank transfer.

🗓️ Services & Appointment Booking

Service listing, time-slot based bookings, booking confirmation, and follow-up tracking.
