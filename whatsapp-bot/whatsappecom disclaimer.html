WhatsApp Integration – Technical Disclaimer

Our platform integrates with WhatsApp to enable customer conversations, order management, and service bookings for small and medium businesses.

This document outlines the differences between WhatsApp Web–based integration and the official WhatsApp Business API, along with our current and future strategy.

Current Integration Approach

In the initial phase, our platform operates using WhatsApp Web–based integration, designed specifically for handling inbound customer conversations such as product inquiries, order confirmations, and service bookings.

WhatsApp Web vs WhatsApp Business API

Aspect WhatsApp Web Integration WhatsApp Business API
Best Suited For Small & medium businesses Large enterprises
Setup Cost Low / Minimal High (setup + provider fees)
Message Cost No per-message cost Per-message pricing
Conversation Type Inbound, transactional conversations Inbound + outbound (templates)
Automation Level Limited & contextual Fully compliant automation
Compliance Unofficial (WhatsApp Web usage) Official & WhatsApp-approved

WhatsApp does not officially support automation through WhatsApp Web. While this approach is commonly used by small businesses, there is a potential risk of number restrictions if misused.

Risk Mitigation Strategy

To minimize operational risk, our platform follows strict usage guidelines:

  • Focus on inbound customer-initiated conversations only
  • No bulk or promotional message broadcasting
  • Human-like response timing and controlled automation
  • Designed for real business workflows (orders & bookings)

This usage pattern closely aligns with normal WhatsApp Web business behavior, significantly reducing the likelihood of account restrictions.

Future Transition Strategy

As the platform scales and enterprise clients onboard, we plan to offer official WhatsApp Business API integration as an optional upgrade.

This phased approach allows us to serve SMBs cost-effectively today, while remaining compliant and scalable for enterprise use cases.

Conclusion

Our current integration strategy balances affordability, usability, and operational safety for small businesses, while maintaining a clear roadmap toward official API adoption as the business grows.
