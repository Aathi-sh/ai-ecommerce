Project Resources, Cost Structure & Execution Overview

This document provides a transparent overview of the resources, expected market cost, actual execution model, post-deployment requirements, and the rationale behind the funding expectation for the WhatsApp Commerce & Service Booking Platform.

1. General Resources Required for This Type of Project

A production-ready WhatsApp-based commerce and booking platform typically requires the following resources:

  • Product architecture & system design
  • Backend development (workflows, integrations, data handling)
  • Frontend & admin dashboard development
  • UI/UX design (Figma, user flows)
  • Quality assurance & testing
  • Infrastructure, deployment & monitoring

3. Actual Execution Model Used

This project was executed using a lean, in-house model:

  • Team Lead & Senior Developer (6+ years experience)
    System architecture, backend development, WhatsApp integration, deployment, infrastructure, and product decisions.
  • 1 Developer
    Dashboard development, feature implementation, booking and order workflows.

As a result, approximately 95% of the platform is already developed, including all core business functionality.

No external agency, full design team, or large development staff was used. Major technical and product responsibilities were handled internally.

4. Cost Optimization & Resource Reduction

  • UI/UX flows designed internally instead of hiring a full-time designer
  • Product planning and prioritization done during development
  • No separate paid Team Lead role (handled internally)
  • Testing performed during development cycles

This approach reduced development cost significantly while maintaining execution speed and functional quality.

5. Post-Deployment Requirements (After Client Go-Live)

After deployment to client businesses, the platform requires ongoing operational and technical support:

  • Bug fixes and stability updates
  • Minor feature improvements based on real usage
  • Performance optimization
  • Infrastructure monitoring and backups
  • Customer onboarding support and issue resolution
  • Periodic upgrades and security updates

6. Funding Expectation

Based on the execution completed so far, the significant cost savings achieved, and the current near-completion status of the platform, we are seeking an initial funding of ₹1.5 lakhs.

This funding will be used for final stabilization, post-deployment support, early customer onboarding, and operational readiness, rather than core product development.
